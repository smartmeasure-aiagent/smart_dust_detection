Sensor wiring for the AJ-SR04M

Unplug USB before touching any wire. Wire it exactly as drawn, run the four meter checks, then power up. Pin names come from the board's own silkscreen: if your ESP32 labels differ, match by name (D5 / GPIO5, D18 / GPIO18, VIN / 5V), not by position.

The divider, and why

The sensor drives ECHO at about 5 V; an ESP32 input tolerates 3.3 V. Two resistors scale it down:

ECHO ──[ R1 1 kΩ ]──●── GPIO 18
                     │
                 [ R2 2 kΩ ]
                     │
                    GND

V(GPIO18) = 5 V × 2k / (1k + 2k) = 3.33 V

Colour bands, read from the end without the gold band:

1 kΩbrown · black · red · gold
2 kΩred · black · red · gold
10 Ωbrown · black · black · gold: wrong value

No 2 kΩ? Put two 1 kΩ in series for R2. Other pairs that work (R1 / R2): 2.2 kΩ / 3.3 kΩ → 3.0 V, 10 kΩ / 20 kΩ → 3.33 V, 1 kΩ / 1.5 kΩ → 3.0 V.

Meter checks before power

USB unplugged, multimeter on resistance (Ω):

  1. GPIO 18 pin to GND: about 2 kΩ. Near 0 Ω means a short or the 1 Ω / 2.2 Ω parts are still in.
  2. Sensor ECHO pin to GPIO 18 pin: about 1 kΩ. Open (OL) means R1 is not in the same row as the junction.
  3. Sensor TRIG pin to D5 pin: about 0 Ω.

Then plug USB in, meter on DC volts:

  1. Sensor 5V to sensor GND: 4.6–5.1 V. Around 3.3 V means it is on the 3V3 pin; 0 V means VIN or GND is not connected.
What the ESP32 reported on 1 Nov

GPIO 18 read LOW even with the chip's internal pull-up switched on, and ECHO did not go HIGH once in 30 pings (including a 1 ms trigger). The pin is held to ground and the module never answers. In order of likelihood:

  1. Low-value resistors (1 Ω / 2.2 Ω) in the divider short ECHO to ground. Meter check 1 catches this.
  2. TRIG and ECHO swapped, so the module never receives a trigger.
  3. Sensor 5V wired to 3V3, or VIN not connected. Meter check 4 catches this.
  4. Probe cable loose, or the R19 mode pad fitted with a resistor (it should be empty for trigger/echo mode on most AJ-SR04M boards).
How you know it works

Aim the probe at a wall or the floor at least 25 cm away (closer is inside its blind zone). Within a few seconds the server terminal prints:

bin-01: sensor recovered
Reading: {'distance': 61.3, 'fill': 0, 'status': 'AVAILABLE', ...}

and the dashboard's Data pipeline panel turns fully green. No restart needed.
